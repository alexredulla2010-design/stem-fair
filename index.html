KamoTech T.O.P.S. Pre Order

KamoTech T.O.P.S. serves as a two-in-one tool that measures liquid pH and detects food spoilage in high-protein perishables derived from kamote tops. The product will change color depending on the acidity or alkalinity of a substance. The product will also serve as an indicator of food spoilage, as the growth of microorganisms in food alters its acidity or alkalinity.
